Our Heritage

Craft that carries a centuries-old story

Among the Maasai, every colour tells a story — red for courage, blue for the sky and energy, green for the land, white for peace, orange for hospitality. When you hold a piece of Maasai beadwork, you hold a language of colour passed down through generations.

At Hope Gift Crafts, we work directly with artisans — mothers, fathers and young makers across Kenya — paying fair prices so their craft thrives and reaches the world with pride.

  • Direct from artisansNo middlemen — fair pay goes straight to the makers.
  • Authentic materialsGenuine glass beads, leather and hand-dyed fabric.
  • Export-ready qualityEvery piece inspected and packaged for safe global travel.
